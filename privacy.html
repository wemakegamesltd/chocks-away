Chocks Away! - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026

Chocks Away! is made by We Make Games Ltd.

The game does not collect, store, sell or share any personal information. It has no adverts, no accounts, no analytics and no tracking, and it does not need an internet connection.

If you buy the full game, the purchase is handled entirely by Google Play. We never see your name, your payment details or your Google account; the game only asks Google Play whether the full game has been bought on this account.

Your progress (missions unlocked, stars, scores, pub records) and your settings (sound, music, vibration) are saved only on your own device. They are deleted if you uninstall the game, and you can also wipe them at any time from Options > Reset Progress.

Music by Alex Morgan.

The game uses the phone's vibration only if you leave the Vibration option switched on.

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page.
